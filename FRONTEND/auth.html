Forgot password?

  1. Type your username (not your email) and password. Click "Log In" once and wait — repeated clicks won't speed it up and may temporarily lock you out for a minute (max 5 tries/min).
  2. Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password?" below and enter the email you registered with.
  3. Click "Send Reset Code" once. A 6-digit code arrives by email within a minute (check spam if not). The code lasts 15 minutes and allows 5 attempts.
  4. Enter the code and a new password (at least 8 characters, with both letters and numbers), then click "Reset Password" once.
  5. Log in normally with your username and new password.
